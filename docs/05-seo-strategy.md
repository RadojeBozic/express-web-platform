# Express Web Platform — SEO Strategy

## Objectives
Ensure important pages are crawlable/indexable, align them with real search intent, build topical authority and turn organic visits into meaningful actions.

## Intent model
Informational content belongs in guides; commercial investigation in service/pricing pages; transactional intent in focused contact/landing pages; project searches in the projects section.

## Technical baseline
Verify HTTPS/canonical host, robots, sitemap, canonicals, status codes, redirects, multilingual URLs and `hreflang`, Vue rendering/indexability, Core Web Vitals, structured data, Search Console and image handling.

## Metadata
Every indexable page needs a unique title/description, canonical, Open Graph data, correct robots directive, one H1 and meaningful headings.

## Structured data
Use only types matching visible maintained content, such as `Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Article`, `Service` and carefully justified `FAQPage`.

## Topic clusters
Business websites, online stores/sales, digital marketing, practical AI, business applications/digitalisation and project/case-study content. Each cluster needs a pillar page, supporting articles, internal links and a measurable CTA.

## Blog quality
Answer a specific question, include original practical reasoning, avoid generic filler, disclose limits, add a suitable CTA/internal links and review outdated claims.

## Measurement
Indexed pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, landing-page engagement, organic conversions, branded/non-branded queries and content-assisted inquiries.

## Immediate audit
Confirm indexability/canonical, inspect sitemap/robots, test route rendering, inventory metadata, verify multilingual implementation, find duplicate/thin pages and standardise blog CTAs/internal links.
