# Express Web Platform — Roadmap

## Principles
Stabilise before promotion, prioritise measurable outcomes, keep tasks reviewable, use evidence and avoid starting several high-risk changes simultaneously.

## Status vocabulary
`Proposed`, `Planned`, `Ready`, `In progress`, `Review`, `Blocked`, `Done`, `Deferred`, `Cancelled`.

## Phase 0 — Documentation and audit
**Goal:** Reliable technical and product baseline.  
**Exit:** architecture is verified, unknowns are listed, backlog is prioritised and deployment/rollback are understood.

## Phase 1 — Production readiness and trust
Pricing review, blog CTAs, contact journey, visual consistency, accessibility, SEO/indexing, analytics baseline, performance and privacy review.

## Phase 2 — Newsletter and content distribution
Subscription, consent, confirmation, subscriber storage, provider integration, queues, welcome email, new-post notification, unsubscribe and delivery metrics.

## Phase 3 — Analytics and conversion optimisation
Event taxonomy, conversion goals, campaign attribution, dashboards, CTA/page experiments and funnel analysis.

## Phase 4 — Chatbot knowledge and lead qualification
Approved sources, FAQ/services/pricing/projects, fallback, escalation, human handoff, analytics and evaluation set.

## Phase 5 — Campaign and landing-page system
Reusable landing pages, focused offers, lead capture, attribution, social/Google Ads readiness and case studies.

## Phase 6 — AI-assisted tools
Project brief assistant, website/SEO analysis, service recommendation, proposal support and content workflows.

## Phase 7 — Platform/SaaS expansion
Client workspace, maintenance dashboards, analytics summaries, reusable vertical solutions and GBS integrations, driven only by validated demand.
