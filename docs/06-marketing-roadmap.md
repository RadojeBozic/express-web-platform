# Express Web Platform — Marketing Roadmap

## Principle
Promotion begins with a ready product, measurable goals and a defined follow-up process.

## Readiness gates
Core pages and pricing are coherent, CTAs/forms work, lead ownership is defined, analytics is active, mobile/SEO/privacy checks pass and recent content has appropriate CTAs.

## Funnel
```text
Awareness -> Consideration -> Intent -> Qualification -> Proposal -> Delivery -> Retention
```
Each stage needs its own content and metric.

## Content engine
One approved article may become a newsletter, LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram content, FAQ entries, chatbot knowledge and sales support. Adapt content to each channel rather than copying it unchanged.

## Channel roles
Website: canonical offer/conversion. LinkedIn: professional insight. Facebook: local/approachable education. Instagram: visual storytelling. Newsletter: permission-based relationship. Google Search/Ads: measured high-intent acquisition.

## Campaign sequence
Organic update, supporting content, analytics baseline, small message test, focused landing page, limited paid test, review lead quality, improve before scaling.

## KPI groups
Awareness, engagement, intent and commercial quality. Qualified leads, proposals, accepted projects and acquisition cost matter more than vanity metrics.

## Lead handling
Document receiver, response process, qualification questions, lead notes, follow-up cadence, lost reasons and privacy retention.

## Budget discipline
Start small, define stop conditions, compare lead quality, record creative/audience/page/date and preserve learnings from failed tests.

## Initial workstreams
Readiness checklist, analytics event plan, social announcement package, newsletter launch plan, first focused offer page, case-study format and monthly review.
